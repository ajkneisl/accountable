<p align="center"><strong>Goals get done when friends are watching.</strong></p>

Beacc tracks your goals automatically from the apps you already use, then puts you up against your friends.

## Goals

Pick something you want to do more of (or less of), set a target, and Beacc keeps score for you.

| Source | Goals you can set |
| --- | --- |
| **Steps** (Apple Health) | Walk 10,000 steps a day |
| **Distance** (Apple Health) | Walk or run 3 miles a day, in miles or kilometres |
| **Sleep** (Apple Health) | Sleep 7 hours a night |
| **Workouts** (Apple Fitness) | Work out 4 times a week, or burn a set number of calories |
| **Screen Time** | Stay under 3 hours a day |
| **GitHub** | Make 5 commits a week |
| **LeetCode** | Solve easy, medium or hard problems |
| **Chess.com** | Play a set number of games, or win them |

Goals are **daily** (every day, or just the days you pick) or **weekly** (hit it by Sunday). Each one shows the week day by day and whether you're on pace, and you can look back at earlier weeks.

Every day you meet all your goals extends your **streak**.

## Habits

Habits are the things no app can track, like reading or stretching. Check one off each day and Beacc keeps a heatmap of every day you showed up, along with your current streak.

## Competitions

Create a competition, share its code, and your friends join in seconds. The owner picks the shared goals, and everyone is measured against the same ones.

- **Weekly** standings rank everyone by how many goals they're on pace for this week, with streak as the tie-breaker.
- **All time** counts the weeks each person has finished first.
- **Goals** show each person's progress side by side.

The owner can rename the competition, change its goals, reset the join code, remove members or hand it to someone else.
